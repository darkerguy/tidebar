Wind range

Wind explanation

If input in km/h feels too weird to you, here's how different wind speeds feel.

  • 0-2 km/h • Dead air. No wind, but the air might feel too heavy, still or boring.
  • 3-6 km/h • Light air. There is some wind, but not much. Leaves sway.
  • 7-12 km/h • Breeze. This is the wind many people like, towels slightly move, waves exist but aren't very big.
  • 13-18 km/h • Fresh wind. Like breeze, but unsecured towels might wiggle and slightly move, with waves slightly picking up.
  • 19-26 km/h • Moderate wind. Insecure stuff might start travelling alone, and the wind starts being very noticable.
  • 27-35 km/h • Strong wind. Unless you place rocks on it, your towel will try to escape.
  • 36+ km/h • Insane wind. If you want to keep your belongings, be careful so they don't fly away into another country.
This is not Beaufort scale. It's just how I see the winds.
