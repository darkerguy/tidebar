About Tidebar

Some info about Tidebar so you know what it is

Tidebar is a free, privacy-friendly beach app. It has weather, tides, sun, customizable themes, a map of beaches, 350+ beaches and more cool stuff.

It never requires accounts, sends analytics, or stores cookies. Everything is in localStorage (just a way to store data locally) and isn't sent to any server. You can read the ToS for more details on privacy.

Wallpapers are stock photos (credits), but the logo is made by me.

This isn't my first beach weather app, and probably also not my last - check out the history page!
